We collect as little as we can and we do not sell any of it. This page explains exactly what we hold and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and a password managed by our identity provider. We never see or store your password.
  • Application profile. Patient care hours, GPA, prerequisite status, and target programs — whatever you enter to use the list builder.
  • Payment records. Stripe processes all payments. We receive a confirmation and the last four digits of your card. Card numbers never touch our servers.
  • Usage data. Cookieless analytics: pages viewed, roughly where in the world, nothing that identifies you personally.

What we do not collect

Date of birth, home address, phone number, government identifiers, transcripts, or any health information about you. If a feature does not need a field, the field does not exist.

Aggregated statistics

We publish statistics about admissions outcomes. These are always aggregated, never attributed, and we suppress any figure drawn from fewer than five people so no individual can be identified. By using the service you agree we may include your anonymized outcome data in these published aggregates.

Who we share with

Only the vendors needed to run the service — our host, Stripe for payments, our identity provider, and our email provider. Each operates under a data processing agreement. We do not sell or share personal data with PA programs, recruiters, or advertisers.

Your rights

Whatever state or country you are in, you can access, correct, export, or delete everything we hold about you. Account → Data handles all four without contacting us. Deletion is permanent and takes effect within 30 days across backups.

Retention

Profile data is kept while your account is active and for 12 months after it lapses, then deleted. Payment records are kept for seven years because tax law requires it.

Contact

Privacy questions go to privacy@[yourdomain].com. We answer within five business days.
