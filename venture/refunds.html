Refund Policy

Template · not yet in force
Template only. A published, specific refund policy is your primary defence in a card chargeback. Make sure the terms below match what you will actually honour.

Fourteen days, no questions

Email refunds@[yourdomain].com within 14 days of purchase and we refund you in full. You do not need to give a reason. We do not run a retention script.

How long it takes

We process within two business days. Your bank usually takes a further five to ten to post it. The refund returns to the card you paid with; we cannot send it elsewhere.

After fourteen days

Cycle Pass purchases are not refundable after day 14, because by then you have had the full database. Monthly subscribers can cancel at any time in one click and keep access through the period already paid for; we do not refund partial months.

If something is wrong with the data

If we published a program requirement incorrectly and it affected your application decisions, contact us whatever the date. These are handled individually and we do not hide behind the 14-day window for our own errors.

Before you file a chargeback

Email us first. We have never refused a refund inside the window, and a chargeback costs us a fee and costs you several weeks longer than just asking.
